Questions

Everything you need to know

Who qualifies for PhoneFlex?

Any registered MTN MoMo user aged 18 or older with a valid National Registration Card (NRC). Your MoMo wallet must be active and in good standing.

How much deposit do I need to pay?

Deposits start from as little as 10% of the phone's cash price. The exact amount is shown on every phone before you apply, so there are no surprises.

How are the daily instalments collected?

Instalments are automatically deducted from your linked MTN MoMo wallet each day. You'll get an SMS confirmation after every deduction, and you can top up your wallet any time.

What happens if I miss a payment?

Your wallet is retried automatically. If payments remain outstanding, your account is temporarily locked until the balance is settled. Persistent non-payment may affect your ability to use PhoneFlex again.

Can I pay off my phone early?

Yes. You can settle your full outstanding balance at any time through the MoMo menu — with no extra charges or penalties.

Where do I collect my phone?

Once approved, you can collect your device from your nearest participating MTN service centre, or choose to have it delivered to your address.
